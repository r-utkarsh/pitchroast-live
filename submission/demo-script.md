## Recording notes

- Keep the cursor movement slow and deliberate; click targets are generous.
- The two reviewers must use **separate browser contexts** (private window or another profile), otherwise they share the same identity.
- Do not show `.env`, the App Secret, the Nexconn console secret, or any token. The public App Key is fine, but it is not needed on screen.
- If a connection banner appears, wait for **Live** before continuing. Do not record over the error.
- The five-minute timer will not finish during the recording; say "locks when it ends" rather than waiting for it.
- End on the exported Markdown or the mobile room, not on a settings screen.
